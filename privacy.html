Privacy

Privacy policy

Effective November 5, 2026

Train of Thought is a private journal. Your thoughts belong to you. The app has no accounts, advertising, analytics, tracking, or third-party SDKs, and it does not collect, sell, or share personal data.

Where your thoughts are stored

Everything you write, including text, photos, links, and the connections between thoughts, is stored on your iPhone. If you're signed in to iCloud, it also syncs through your own iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit private database. The developer cannot see or access that data. Apple's handling of iCloud data is described in the Apple Privacy Policy.

Photos and camera

The app only sees the photos you pick in the system photo picker. If you take a photo from the app, the camera is used only to capture that photo, which is saved with your thought.

Dictation

If you use the app's microphone button, your speech is turned into text on your iPhone using Apple's built-in speech recognition. No audio is recorded or saved, and nothing you say is sent to the developer. Microphone access is optional and can be turned off in iOS Settings.

Link previews

When a thought contains a link, the app fetches that page's title and preview image directly from the website, the same way Messages does. That request goes from your iPhone to the website. Nothing is sent to the developer.

Deleting your data

Deleting a thought removes it from your iPhone and your iCloud. To remove everything, delete the app, then delete its data under Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage.

Children's privacy

Because Train of Thought does not collect personal information, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Policy changes

If the app's data practices change, this policy will be updated before the affected version is released.
