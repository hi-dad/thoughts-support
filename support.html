Support

Something not working the way you expect?

Quick fixes

  • Thoughts aren't syncing between devices: check that each device is signed in to the same Apple ID, and that iCloud is on for the app under Settings → [your name] → iCloud. The app's own Settings screen shows whether iCloud Sync is on.
  • A train disappeared from the home screen: trains you haven't added to for a week move to the Shelf at the bottom of the home screen. Add a thought and it comes back.
  • No microphone button: dictation in the app needs iOS 26 or later. You can always use the microphone on the iPhone keyboard instead.
  • Camera or microphone won't open: allow access under Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera or Microphone.
  • Want to branch a thought: open a train, switch to Map, tap the card you want to build on, and add your thought.
